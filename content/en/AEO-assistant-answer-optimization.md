## Metrics to track

Track **qualified organic sessions**, **conversion to meaningful activation**, **trial-to-paid conversion**, **customer retention**, and **fully loaded acquisition costs**. For AI answer visibility, use repeated prompt samples and record model, date, prompt, and citations. Do not use one screenshot as a robust benchmark.

A useful dashboard links content effort to business outcomes: page or prompt group → discovery → qualified engagement → conversion → retention. Segment these metrics by intent; otherwise, a spike in low-intent impressions can obscure deteriorating commercial performance.
